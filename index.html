TUNG TUNGSHOOTER

Tactical shooter — runs entirely in your browser

Survival in Sahur Village. Hold off endless rounds of Tung Tung zombies and defend the Golden Tung Tung statue: it moves to a new room every round, and if the zombies break it the game is over. Press N when you are ready for the next round. Every round you survive opens more of the village. Guns come in four rarities and only come out of gun cases (2000 each, CS odds: the good ones are rare): buy them from the vending machine and open them in your inventory (I). The Gun Shop, where you spawn, sells ammo. Spend the rest on perks and four levels of Pack-a-Punch.
